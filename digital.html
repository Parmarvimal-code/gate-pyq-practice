Question 1

In a system, numbers are represented using 4-bit two's complement form.

N1 = 1011
N2 = 1101
N3 = 1010
N4 = 1001

Which operations result in arithmetic overflow?

A) N1 + N2
B) N2 + N3
C) N3 − N4
D) N1 + N4

Concept

Arithmetic overflow occurs when the result exceeds the representable range.

For 4-bit two's complement:
Range = -8 to +7

Question 4

Consider the 8-bit signed integers X, Y and Z represented using sign-magnitude form.

X = 10110100
Y = 01001100

Which operations result in arithmetic overflow?

A) Z = X + Y
B) Z = X − Y
C) Z = −X + Y
D) Z = −X − Y

Concept

Sign-Magnitude Representation

MSB = Sign Bit
0 → Positive
1 → Negative

Range of 8-bit Sign Magnitude
= -127 to +127
