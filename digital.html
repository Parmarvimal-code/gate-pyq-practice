Question 1 (MSQ | 1 Mark)

In a system, numbers are represented using 4-bit two's complement form. Consider four numbers:

N1 = 1011
N2 = 1101
N3 = 1010
N4 = 1001

Which operations result in arithmetic overflow?

A) N1 + N2
B) N2 + N3
C) N3 − N4
D) N1 + N4

Concept

Arithmetic overflow occurs when the result goes outside the representable range. For 4-bit two's complement:

Range = -8 to +7

If result becomes less than -8 or greater than +7, overflow occurs.

Question 2 (NAT | 1 Mark)

The 32-bit IEEE 754 single precision representation of a number is 0xC2710000

The decimal value is _______ (round off to two decimal places)


Concept

IEEE 754 Single Precision Format

1 bit = Sign
8 bits = Exponent
23 bits = Mantissa

Formula:
Value = (-1)^Sign × Mantissa × 2^(Exponent-127)
